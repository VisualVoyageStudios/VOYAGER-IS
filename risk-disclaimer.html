2. Trading and investing carries risk of loss

Trading financial instruments — including forex, CFDs, indices, and cryptocurrency — carries a real risk of losing some or all of the capital placed at risk. Prices can move quickly and unpredictably. Nothing on this site, and no Voyager product, removes or reduces that risk.

Only trade with capital you can afford to lose. Voyager products are not designed for, and should not be used with, money required for essential living expenses, debt repayment, or any other obligation.

3. Voyager products are analysis tools, not signals or advice

Every Voyager indicator and strategy is a piece of software that displays information on a chart. None of them tell you when to buy or sell, and none of them are a recommendation to take any specific trade. Where a strategy includes built-in entry, exit, or risk logic, that logic is a framework you can test and use — it is still not a guarantee of any outcome, and you remain responsible for every trade you place.

Voyager is not licensed as a Financial Services Provider under South Africa's Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services (FAIS) Act, and nothing provided constitutes financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

4. Past and backtested performance has limits

Where a strategy is described as "backtest-ready," any performance shown from backtesting is hypothetical — it did not involve real capital and does not account for real-world factors like slippage, execution delays, changing spreads, or emotional decision-making under live conditions. Backtested results can also be affected by curve-fitting: a system tuned to perform well on historical data is not guaranteed to perform the same way going forward.

Past performance of any tool, on any market, at any time, is not a reliable indicator of future results.

5. Software and platform limitations

Voyager tools are built and tested carefully, but no software is free of risk:

  • Indicators and strategies can contain bugs, and may behave unexpectedly on certain instruments, timeframes, or under unusual market conditions not anticipated during development.
  • All tools rely on TradingView's platform and data feeds. Outages, data delays, or changes to TradingView's platform are outside Voyager's control and can affect how a tool displays or performs.
  • A tool's output depends on the exchange or broker data feed selected on your chart. Different data feeds for the same instrument can produce different results.
  • Your own broker's execution — spreads, slippage, requotes, and fill quality — will differ from anything shown on a TradingView chart.

If you believe a tool is behaving incorrectly, use the "Report an issue" link on the product card so it can be looked into.

6. Market-specific risks

Leverage (forex and CFDs). Leveraged products can produce losses that exceed your initial deposit. A small adverse price movement can have an outsized effect on a leveraged position.

Volatility (cryptocurrency). Cryptocurrency markets can move sharply in short periods, including outside of typical trading hours, and can gap significantly between sessions.

Liquidity and sessions. Tools that reference session ranges, liquidity zones, or volume profiles are built around typical market behaviour. Thinly-traded instruments, low-liquidity periods, and major news events can all behave very differently from what a tool's historical logic assumes.
