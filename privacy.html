Privacy Policy

How Voyager collects, uses, and protects customer and order information.

Last updated: 2 July 2026 Voyager
Note: This is a practical template, not legal advice. Review it for your own business and jurisdiction before launch.

1. What we collect

We collect information needed to process orders and grant TradingView access:

  • Email address
  • TradingView username
  • Order and payment details from the checkout provider
  • Messages sent via email or WhatsApp

2. How we use it

  • To process purchases and verify payment
  • To grant and manage TradingView invite-only script access
  • To provide support
  • To prevent fraud, chargebacks, or unauthorised sharing
  • To comply with legal, tax, and payment provider requirements

3. Payment information

Card and payment details are processed by Paystack. We do not store card numbers.

4. Third-party services

We may use third-party services for checkout, hosting, email, and messaging. Those providers operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Data retention

Order and access records are kept as long as needed to provide access, handle support, and meet legal and accounting requirements.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information where legally applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

7. POPIA — Protection of Personal Information Act

Voyager processes personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). This section sets out how POPIA specifically applies to your information.

Responsible party. Voyager is the responsible party for personal information collected through this site, as defined under POPIA.

Lawful basis for processing. We process your information because it is necessary to perform a contract with you (processing your order and granting access), to comply with a legal obligation (tax and accounting records), or on the basis of your consent (e.g. joining the update waitlist).

Your rights under POPIA. Subject to applicable exceptions, you have the right to:

  • Be notified that your personal information is being collected
  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate, outdated, or unlawfully processed information
  • Object to processing of your personal information in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (e.g. unsubscribe from update emails at any time)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your information has been mishandled

Information Officer. Requests relating to your rights under POPIA can be directed to our Information Officer at visualvoyagerbsn@gmail.com.

Information Regulator. If you feel your POPIA rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa via inforegulator.org.za.

Cross-border transfers. Some third-party providers we use (such as payment processors or hosting) may process information outside South Africa. Where this happens, we rely on those providers maintaining an adequate level of data protection consistent with POPIA.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: visualvoyagerbsn@gmail.com
